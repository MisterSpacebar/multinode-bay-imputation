SECTION 1 — ST-GNN IMPUTATION RESULTS (FIGURES 01–07)
========================================================================
The Spatial-Temporal Graph Neural Network (ST-GNN) was trained on
5-minute water quality readings from 7 stations across North and
South Biscayne Bay (2025-03-10 to 2026-08-01, ~146,000 timesteps).
It imputes sensor gaps using readings at neighbouring stations,
air temperature and net water balance as external forcing.

SECTION 2 — FUZZY COGNITIVE MAP: PHYSICAL FCM (FIGURES 08–11)
========================================================================
The Physical FCM uses 11 concepts (3 atmospheric forcing + 8 sensor
features) at daily resolution (2025-2026). It learns which variables
causally drive which others via Ridge regression with a 1-day lag
(selected by cross-validated lag search over lags 1, 2, 3, 7 days).

means 'concept i suppresses concept j'.
PURPOSE: Full causal topology overview at daily resolution.

SECTION 5 — FCM VALIDATION (FIGURES 21–24)
========================================================================
The Nutrient FCM is validated two ways:
  1. One-step-ahead hindcast: plug observed A(t) → predict A(t+1)
     and compare to actual observed A(t+1). Metrics: R², RMSE, MAE.
  2. Event backcasting: seed at a past event, simulate forward,
     overlay on subsequent observations.

APPENDIX — MODEL & DATA QUICK-REFERENCE
========================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
ST-GNN Architecture
------------------------------------------------------------
  • NodeEncoder → SpatialGAT (3 layers) → GRU (2 layers) → Decoder
  • Input dim = n_features + n_features (mask) + n_forcing + 2 (time enc) = 21
  • n_features=8, n_forcing=3 (rain, temp_min, temp_max for imputation)
  • 97,544 trainable parameters
  • Training: 50 epochs, WIN_LEN=72 (6h), STRIDE=36, MASK_RATIO=0.30
  • Val loss: 0.0021  (best checkpoint)

------------------------------------------------------------
FCM Architecture
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Ridge regression with 1-step lag (lag selected by held-out validation)
  • Weights normalised to [-1, 1]; sigmoid activation for simulation
  • Physical FCM: 11 concepts, daily, 2025-2026
  • Nutrient FCM: 16 concepts, monthly, 2021-2026
  • Forcing: net_water (Rain - Hargreaves PET), temp_min, temp_max

------------------------------------------------------------
Dataset Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
  • 7 graph nodes: L0, L1, L2, L6, L7, biscayne_bay, consolidated_crest5
  • Time range: 2025-03-10 to 2026-08-01 (146,421 five-minute steps)
  • Overall missing: 73.2 % (before imputation)
  • Grab samples: 761 surface rows, 20 sites, 2021-09 to 2024-12
  • Buoy data: crest3-5, haulover, kampong, royal (Jun-Jul 2026, 15-min)
